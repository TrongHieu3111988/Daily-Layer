For grown-ups

From screen to shelf in three steps

Your child makes it

They draw, colour or sculpt in the browser. Nothing is uploaded: drawings stay on your device.

You download the files

A coloured OBJ and 3MF for multi-colour printers, plus a one-colour STL. Every file is checked as a closed solid.

Print it at home

Drag the file into Bambu Studio, Orca or PrusaSlicer. Colours match to your filaments automatically.
