Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Daily Layer is made for children and their families, so we collect as little as possible.

Who we are

Daily Layer is a small independent studio based in Vietnam. We decide how the information described here is used. Contact: tronghieu3111988@gmail.com.

Children using the games

  • No sign-up, no names, no chat and no text input in any game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the shop is for parents and other adults to buy from.
  • No advertising, no ad tracking and no analytics. We do not set cookies ourselves.
  • Game progress, settings (such as your language choice), and children's sculptures and drawings are saved only in the browser on your own device (local storage). They are never sent to us. Clearing the browser's site data deletes them. The site may also keep a copy of its own pages in your browser so it works offline.
  • Fonts and code libraries used by the games are hosted by us, so your browser does not contact Google or any content network when you play.
  • If a child has sent us information by mistake, ask a parent to email us and we will delete it.

When you buy something

Checkout is run by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and Merchant of Record. They collect your email address, billing country and payment details to process the order, send your receipt and download link, and meet tax rules. We receive your email address and order details from Lemon Squeezy so we can deliver files and support you; we never see your full card details. See the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy.

We use your email only for your order and membership (receipts, downloads, support). We will not sell it or share it with anyone else, and we will only send news if you choose to sign up for it.

Hosting and other recipients

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver pages and protect against abuse. Lemon Squeezy and Cloudflare are the only outside services that receive information through this site.

How long we keep information

Order records are kept as long as tax, accounting and refund rules require. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you, normally no more than 24 months. Everything else stays on your own device until you clear it.

Where information goes

We are in Vietnam, Lemon Squeezy and Cloudflare operate internationally, and you may be elsewhere, so your order information can be processed in several countries. We only use these services to run the shop.

Your rights and choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, delete, restrict or hand over the information we hold about you, to stop using it, or to withdraw a consent you gave. Email tronghieu3111988@gmail.com. We reply within 2 business days, give you a copy or correct it within 10 days, and delete within 20 days. If a request is complex we may take longer, and we will tell you why. We offer these rights to everyone, including people in the EU, UK, California and Vietnam, and you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security and incidents

We keep only what is needed and rely on Cloudflare and Lemon Squeezy for secure storage and payment. If an incident affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities where the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top shows the latest version.
