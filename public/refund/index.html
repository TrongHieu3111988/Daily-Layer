Refund Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

In short: files are delivered instantly, so purchases are generally final. If a file is broken or not as described, we will fix it or refund you.

Digital files

Because downloads are delivered immediately and cannot be returned, we do not offer refunds for change of mind once a file has been downloaded.

You are entitled to a full refund within 14 days of purchase if:

  • the file is corrupt, will not open, or will not slice in a common slicer (Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Cura, OrcaSlicer), and we cannot send a working fix within 3 days;
  • the file is clearly different from its description or pictures;
  • you were charged twice for the same item, or bought a file that was already included in your active membership.

Memberships

  • You can cancel at any time; you will not be charged again and keep access until the end of the paid month.
  • If you forgot to cancel and have not downloaded anything in the new billing period, write to us within 7 days of the renewal and we will refund that renewal.
  • Partial months are not refunded otherwise.

How to ask for a refund

Email tronghieu3111988@gmail.com with your order number (from the receipt email) and a short description of the problem — a screenshot helps. We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds are issued through Lemon Squeezy to your original payment method and usually arrive in 5–10 business days.

Printing problems

Failed prints caused by printer settings, filament or hardware are not grounds for a refund, but we are happy to help — send us a photo and your slicer settings.
