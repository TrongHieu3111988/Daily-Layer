Print tips

No supports, three parts: box upright, lid top-down (as laid out), bow flat. 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill. Right-hand thread, 3.6 mm pitch, 0.4 mm clearance: screw the lid on gently the first time. Push the bow's peg into the hole on top of the lid (a dab of glue makes it permanent). Inside: 67 mm wide, about 60 mm deep, for small gifts, rolled-up gift cards or treasures. Keep the bow away from children under 3.

Printed toys can have small parts and are not for children under 3. An adult should check each print before play.
